# Subtitle translation teaching files

These original fictional tutorial captions accompany the [subtitle translation guide](../../SUBTITLE-TRANSLATION.md). They are not transcripts from a recording, Valmera-generated subtitles or a translation benchmark. No audio or video is included.

- [English source](tutorial-v1.en.srt)
- [Spanish illustrative draft](tutorial-v1.es.srt)

Both UTF-8 SRT files contain three sequential, non-overlapping cues: 0–2 seconds, 2.2–5.2 seconds and 5.4–8 seconds. They share the same timeline. The Spanish draft has not received independent native-speaker review. Use these files to inspect structure and compare wording, then replace them with reviewed subtitles aligned to your own approved recording.

The sample wording may be copied and adapted for your own editing exercises. Keep the distinction between an illustrative sample and measured product output when describing it.
