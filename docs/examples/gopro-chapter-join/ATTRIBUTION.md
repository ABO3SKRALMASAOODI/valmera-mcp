# Footage credit and license

Cabblers published the original video on Wikimedia Commons on December 9, 2017. [Downhill footage taken with GoPro on a Fullface helmet installed with NINJA MOUNT](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Downhill_footage_taken_with_GoPro_on_a_Fullface_helmet_installed_with_NINJA_MOUNT.ogv)

Valmera resized and normalized the footage to 30 fps, trimmed two versions with different join points, and annotated four extracted frames. The adapted files `continuous.mp4`, `skipped-frames.mp4`, and `chapter-join.jpg` are shared under the same license as the original.
