## Define what counts as delivered

Three events with the same scope produce **three replay MP4s, three SRT files, three chapter lists and nine social MP4s**. Count an event as ready when its replay and supporting files refer to the same approved edit and its clips have each passed review. Use the [editable monthly delivery sheet](MONTHLY-WEBINAR-DELIVERY.csv) to assign the work and record file versions. Each row represents one expected file.

| Deliverable | What's on the approval check |
| --- | --- |
| Full replay | Watch both sides of removed sections. Scrub through the product demo, check slide legibility and confirm the full Q&A is present if required. |
| Replay SRT | Open the actual approved MP4 with the SRT. Check one cue before and after a cut, and one near the end. Confirm important product names and speaker names. |
| Chapter list | Open each chapter in the replay player or seek to its time on the exported file. Check the first spoken words or screen action. |
| Social clips | Play each downloaded file at its intended size. Check that the quote is complete and the product result can be read with sound off. Assign one clip topic or purpose per file. |

The [recorded chapter and subtitle handoff example](EDIT-FULL-WEBINAR-REPLAY-KEEP-CAPTIONS-CHAPTERS.md#run-the-cut-and-check-what-actually-changed) shows how a cut shifts both sets of times. Use the pilot to check the timing in your player and at your publishing size.

## Review a late correction before buying the month

Tell the editor that a number or qualifier changed after the first approval. Locate the line in the replay and in each social clip. If the correction changes the replay duration, recheck its separate SRT and every later chapter start before publishing. A clip that quotes the changed line needs a revised export and approval of its own. If an unaffected clip is still approved, keep its current filename and version in the delivery sheet.

For example, the second webinar's replay and clips 1 and 3 repeat the line that needs a correction. Hold those three MP4s while the editor revises them. Keep clip 2 available for publishing if it contains no version of the same claim. Verify the SRT against the revised replay and record whether chapter times moved.

## Send one brief to each vendor

> We supply three recorded 45-minute webinars per month. Quote a paid pilot for one event, then a recurring monthly schedule. For each event, deliver one edited 16:9 replay MP4, one matching SRT, a chapter list keyed to the approved replay and three captioned social MP4s. Please state the first-draft and final delivery dates, what a revision round covers and who owns the review-to-upload handoff. Explain what changes when two events are recorded in the same week.

Compare quotes at the same volume and review window. Ask for the turnaround after consolidated feedback as well as before the first cut. Confirm file naming, language, review ownership and the destination player when you send the source.
