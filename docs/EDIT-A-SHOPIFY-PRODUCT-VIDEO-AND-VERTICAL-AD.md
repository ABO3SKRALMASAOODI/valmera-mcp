# Edit one product recording for a Shopify gallery and a vertical ad

Make two cuts from one approved recording. In the product gallery, show the starting material, the action and the result in that order. In the 9:16 ad, lead with the result, then show how the product achieved it. A shopper who already opened the product page has a different question from someone scrolling past the ad.

## Play two edits of the same footage

| Delivery | What opens the cut | What a viewer can check with sound off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Landscape product gallery MP4](examples/ecommerce-gallery-and-ad/gallery.mp4) | Whole beans | The beans enter the sequence whole, move while the blender runs, and appear ground at the end. |
| [Portrait ad MP4](examples/ecommerce-gallery-and-ad/vertical-ad.mp4) | Ground coffee | The result is visible first. The cut then shows the starting beans and the motion that connects them to the result. |

These 4.8-second reference edits were made offline from one openly licensed recording. Both MP4s decode to 120 frames at 25 fps and have no audio track. The gallery spends 1.44 seconds on the starting state. The portrait cut gives the result 1.12 seconds at the opening. [Exact source offsets and file hashes](examples/ecommerce-gallery-and-ad/measurements.json)

For your product, start with the shot that answers a buying question. A bag opening might matter for packaging. A tight hand-and-control shot can show how much force something takes. Use the closest framing that still shows the action and keep the result on screen long enough to inspect.

## Route the two edits to the right destination

| Decision | Shopify gallery | Vertical paid ad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product identity | Match the page's SKU and shown option. Keep the defining colour or size visible in the media. | Make the product identifiable from the opening and check that the linked offer applies to the depicted SKU. |
| Format | Upload an MP4 or use a supported embed. Check how the media and thumbnail look in the active storefront theme on mobile. [Shopify product media types](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/product-media-types) | Open the 9:16 file in the actual placement preview. Check that the CTA, subtitles and product detail sit clear of the placement's interface. |
| Approval test | Show the muted video to someone who has only seen the listing. Ask what the product did and which option they saw. | Show just the first two seconds. Ask what result they saw. Then play the rest to check that the clip supports that result. |
| Changes later | If the footage changes, check the product-page media, thumbnail and every cited feature. | Keep time-limited offer text editable. After a change, verify the exported file and landing page together. [Recorded offer removal](PRODUCT-OFFER-REMOVAL-EXAMPLE.md) |

[Shopify's media setup instructions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/add-media) cover uploading and attaching a video. For an editor trial, import one source and make the two exports before committing to the workflow. Check that switching format preserves the useful part of the frame, short text stays readable, and revising one delivery leaves the approved version available.
