# Compare monthly webinar and product demo editing services for UAE teams

If your team records two webinars and two product demos per month, ask each editing service to quote the same set of downloads. Give the vendor your next launch date in Gulf Standard Time, the number of full edits and cutdowns, and a specific revision to include in the pilot. Ask when your team can download the last approved file.

Four recordings and four cutdowns per recording means **four masters, 16 cutdowns and 20 MP4s**. A square version of every vertical cutdown brings the total to 36 MP4s. List separate subtitle files beside their videos so vendors can quote the same approval work. One revision to a product name can affect both formats and the captions for every cut that shows it.

## Copy this quote request

> We record two 45–60 minute webinars and two 10–15 minute product demos each month. Quote one polished landscape master and four captioned vertical cutdowns per recording. Quote an optional square version of each cutdown and separate English and Arabic captions per master. Please specify how many recordings you can edit at once, our intake cutoff, first preview, feedback and final download times in GST (UTC+4), and what counts as a revision. Please give us a separate quote for a pilot with one source recording, one master and one social clip.

Use one real change in the pilot. For example, ask the editor to correct an approved product name in the closing title and captions in both files. Review the change at the end of each video and check the subtitle file if the pilot includes one. Record when your editor receives consolidated notes and when you can download both approved files. Use that interval to schedule your next launch week.
