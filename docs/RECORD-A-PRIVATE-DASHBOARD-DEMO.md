# Record a product demo of a dashboard that requires sign-in

If your product lives behind sign-in, record the workflow in an approved demo workspace, then give the editor the recording. Start the take after opening the workspace and finish on a visible result. The editor needs the footage, a short brief, and the delivery requirements. Your team can keep the login and workspace access.

## Run a four-step rehearsal

Open the [sample workspace file](examples/private-dashboard-demo/sample-workspace.html) on GitHub, choose **Download raw file**, then open it from your downloads. It uses fictional data, works offline, and exports a small CSV to your device.

| Shot | What to record | What the viewer should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Start in the workspace | Hold on **Workspace overview** for a beat. | A viewer knows where to begin. |
| 2. Locate the action | Click **Reports**, then **Open November report**. | Both controls are legible before and during the click. |
| 3. Confirm the result | Hold the report title and the two rows of sample data. | The viewer can identify the report and the sample workspace. |
| 4. Export | Click **Export sample CSV** and hold on the status. | The page shows **November report CSV requested**. Check the actual sample CSV in your downloads separately. |

On your live product, use a workspace your team has approved for recording. Load only demo records, close personal tabs, mute notifications, and verify the browser is capturing the intended window or tab. Rehearse the same inputs and labels you plan to narrate. Start over if a private value appears in an account menu, browser notice or download preview. Check the downloaded edit as well as the source.

## Brief to send with the recording

> Make a 30-second 16:9 walkthrough showing a new admin how to export the sample November report. Keep the workspace opening, the Reports click, the November report title, and the export status in that order. Remove setup and idle time while keeping the control labels legible. Hold on the report and final status long enough to read. Preserve the original voice if the recording includes narration. Show a preview before exporting.

When you review, pause on the two controls, the report title and the final status. If the last click has no payoff, ask for the source frames that show the result and two more seconds on that screen. A polished zoom only helps if viewers can still follow the task.
