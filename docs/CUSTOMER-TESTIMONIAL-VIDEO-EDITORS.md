# AI video editors for customer testimonials from recorded interviews

Choose an editor with one recorded interview and one quote your customer has approved in context. Give every tool the same source and brief. Export a case-study cut and a short version, then compare what each one says about the customer's result. A fast first cut saves time only if the customer can approve the version you intend to publish.

Valmera wrote this guide. The editor shortlist is based on the linked product documentation. The two public practice cuts below show how to inspect a claim. They are examples for comparison across editors.

## Use the same approval test on every tool

> Make a short case-study clip from the supplied interview. Keep the customer's original words around the measured result, including the type of close, when the improvement occurred and the quarter-end exception. Add captions and a lower third where I can supply approved wording. Export an MP4. Then prepare a shorter social version that keeps the same scope. If the target duration leaves too little room, show me the shortest complete version for review.

Play each download with sound, then read the captions with sound off. Check **result**, **scope**, **exception** and **customer attribution** in both versions. Put a timestamp in your review notes whenever something drops out. If a title or caption says “close” while the approved passage says “standard weekly close,” correct the title or caption before sending the cut for approval.

For the final trial, give one revision that a real reviewer could ask for. “Start on the workflow change, keep the approved result and exception, and move the name card clear of the captions.” Record the time to a customer-ready file across the first edit and the revision. The time spent reviewing, correcting and approving both versions is the number to use when deciding whether the tool will save work next month.
