## Run a fair comparison on a recording you own

Use a 45- to 90-second screen recording with a short loading pause, one step you retry and two controls the viewer must locate. Save the same input for both tools. Your first edit brief can be:

> Remove the failed attempt and the wait before the result. Keep the successful click, its control label and the confirmation it opens. Zoom only enough to read the label. Hold on the confirmation for two full seconds. Show me the preview before export.

For the revision, ask to move the zoom later without losing the click or shortening the hold. With the export muted, ask someone unfamiliar with the product to say which control was clicked and what confirmed it worked. Count failures, reviewer minutes and correction rounds through the approved MP4. If most viewers will watch on a phone, do this check on the phone-sized export.
