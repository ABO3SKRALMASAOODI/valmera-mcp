## Price one season of clips

Record the number of months you expect the show to run, whole minutes of footage processed per month, and total clip candidates generated per month. Count rejected candidates too if the provider charges when it makes a clip. Use the [annual clipping plan comparison](https://valmera.io/ai-video-editor-pricing-comparison#annual-clip-plans) to change the workload.

For example, a three-month season with four 100-minute interviews and 40 candidates each month uses 1,200 source minutes and produces 120 candidates. On November 15, 2026, the following US-dollar base offers were each $29 per month or $174 for the year. Three monthly payments total $87 before extras. The plans cover different amounts of work.

| Selected offer | Does one fresh monthly allowance cover this example? | Does the example fit the quoted annual total? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OpusClip Pro, one pack](https://www.opus.pro/pricing) | 400 source minutes exceed 300 credits by 100 each month. Budget for extra processing if you stay monthly. | 1,200 of 3,600 credits. The [clipping credit rules](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/how-are-credits-consumed) describe an upfront annual grant. |
| [Vizard AI Studio Creator](https://vizard.ai/pricing) | 400 of 600 credits for standard clipping. | 1,200 of 7,200 credits. The [one-credit-per-minute rule](https://help.vizard.ai/en/articles/12017624-how-credits-are-used-for-ai-processing) applies to clipping or transcription. |
| [Klap Basic](https://klap.app/pricing) | 40 of 100 generated clips. | 120 of 1,200 candidates. Its pricing FAQ says the annual allotment is issued upfront. |

For Valmera, measure the credits consumed by one representative episode including the revisions and final export. Multiply by the number of similar episodes per active month. Record the actual ending of the downloaded MP4 and whether the client accepts it. [Valmera Plus](https://valmera.io/subscribe) listed $15 monthly or $150 annually with 500 credits refreshed monthly on either billing cycle when checked. The comparison includes a field for your own measurement.
