## Check long-file coverage before timing the edit

Check the file size, source duration and upload speed before opening your paid pilot. For example, an 18 GB upload at a sustained 20 Mbps takes about two hours before overhead. Keep transfer, preparation, review and export times in separate columns. Ask for a source timestamp near the end and click or seek to it before approving the cut. [Valmera’s local file and timestamp checker](https://valmera.io/tools/long-video-upload-checker)

The public clock fixture is a sparse, silent four-hour MP4. The same visible time, **03:20:00**, appears at **03:20:00** in the original and **00:20:00** in the final-hour excerpt. The downloaded files decode to four hours and one hour respectively. This checks the offset after splitting. For a production test, use your actual camera file and check both picture and audio near the end. [Original clock MP4](https://valmera.io/examples/long-recording/source-clock.mp4) · [Final-hour excerpt](https://valmera.io/examples/long-recording/final-hour.mp4) · [Original caption times](https://valmera.io/examples/long-recording/source-clock.srt)

## Approve the master and two derivatives

1. List a consequential early claim, a middle passage you expect the editor to trim, and a late answer such as one at **01:21:00**. Include any qualifier or question that the answer depends on. Give every editor the same source and brief.
2. Ask for an episode preview. Listen at the joins around each protected passage and confirm the late answer is in the master. Save its approved version before starting dependent clips.
3. Ask for two different clips based on the approved episode. Record their source times and what must stay audible. Revise one of the two, then confirm that the approved master and the other clip still match their files.
4. Measure the time to all three approved downloads, active review minutes, and correction rounds. Watch the actual MP4s and check captions, framing and sound. If your team needs a project rather than a video file, test one real timeline handoff separately.
