## Decide what counts as a finished asset

For eight products, two markets and two formats, request **32 approved MP4s per month**. Specify which placements need their own cuts, such as a landscape product page and a portrait paid ad. Ask for a separate UTF-8 SRT for each approved timeline that needs selectable captions, and specify where you need burned-in captions. If two versions share the same approved timing and wording, they can reference the same SRT.

Freeze the shots, timing and product spelling on the shared master. Approve changes to the amount, currency, market-specific graphics, call to action and destination independently. If the source image has a price or English label baked into it, send the clean shot or specify the replacement in the brief. Tell the editor who will approve Arabic wording and who owns the source of truth for offers.

## Run one product through the full handoff

1. Supply one approved product folder, one copy of the current offer sheet and one reviewed Arabic glossary. Identify the first and last frame of the local end card.
2. Request the UAE and Saudi cuts in portrait. Watch both in the placement preview with its overlays. Check the brand and SKU spelling, Arabic word order, amount, currency, action text and end-card hold. Open the destination URLs separately and check that each is the approved market and product.
3. Change one Saudi offer and ask for a single replacement file with a new version number. Check the corrected downloaded file, then verify the UAE deliverable still matches its approved version. Update the delivery sheet with both filenames and approval dates.
