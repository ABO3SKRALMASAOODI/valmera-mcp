# Make a photo slideshow with music and revise one shot

If you have photos and a song and need a downloadable MP4, you can finish a short slideshow in Valmera’s free browser editor without an account. The photos and soundtrack stay on the device. Use an eligible paid Studio account when you want an AI editor to work from the sequence and take revisions in chat. [Open the slideshow maker](https://valmera.io/tools/ai-slideshow-maker#photo-sequence).

## Try one specific change

Use the three Apollo 11 photographs already loaded in the editor. Export the first cut, move the lunar module one place earlier, and export again. The filenames in the source archive match those in the Studio brief. [Download the NASA photos and credits](https://valmera.io/examples/photo-slideshow/apollo-photos.zip).

| Playback position | First cut | After moving the lunar module earlier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:02 | Earthrise | Earthrise |
| 0:02 to 0:03 | Bootprint | Lunar module |
| 0:03 to 0:05 | Bootprint | Bootprint |
| 0:05 to 0:06 | Lunar module | Bootprint |

Both reference files were exported from the browser editor. At 0:02 the revision should show the module. At 0:05 it should still show the bootprint. The module gets one second and the bootprint gets three seconds in either order. [Download the first MP4](https://valmera.io/examples/photo-slideshow/apollo-first.mp4) · [Download the revised MP4](https://valmera.io/examples/photo-slideshow/apollo-revised.mp4).

## Use your photos and soundtrack

1. Choose **Use my photos** to replace the sample. Select up to 12 JPEG, PNG or WebP photos. Use **Earlier** or **Later** to set the order.
2. Pick landscape (1280 × 720), portrait (720 × 1280), or square (960 × 960). For a group portrait, select **Show whole photo** and check both outside faces. **Crop to fill** centers the photo and trims its edges.
3. Set individual holds in quarter-second increments, up to two minutes in total. A gentle push-in is optional. Add your soundtrack if you have one. A short track ends in silence, and a long track plays only under the sequence.
4. Choose **Create slideshow MP4**, play through a transition, then download. The browser needs H.264 encoding support. Desktop Chrome or Edge is a good place to try if an export reports that the codec is unavailable.

## Hand the sequence to the AI editor

Choose **Use this sequence in Studio** to carry the filenames, order, holds, framing and soundtrack instructions into the editing brief. Attach the original files in Studio. For the sample, unpack the source ZIP and attach the three JPEGs. After reviewing the preview, ask for a revision in terms you can check at playback, such as “Move lunar-module.jpg to second place. Keep its one-second hold and keep Earthrise first.” [AI editing access and pricing](https://valmera.io/pricing).
