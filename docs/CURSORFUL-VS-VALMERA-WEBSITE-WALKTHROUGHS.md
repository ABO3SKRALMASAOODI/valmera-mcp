# Cursorful vs Valmera for a website walkthrough MP4

Choose the recorder based on who will perform the clicks. Cursorful documents recording as you operate the browser or an app, adding zooms from your interactions, and editing that recording. Valmera can open a public URL, follow a written sequence in its browser and assemble the recording into a video you can revise. For an account-only flow, record the approved screen yourself and bring that footage to Valmera.

## Choose by starting point and delivery

| Starting point | Cursorful | Valmera |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A public URL and a list of steps | Record the flow yourself with the browser extension. The automatic zooms follow interactions while you record. [Cursorful recorder](https://cursorful.com/features#recording) | Supply the URL, actions and expected result to the website recorder. Play the capture before editing it. [Valmera website recorder](https://valmera.io/tools/record-website-demo) |
| A take from your dashboard or desktop | Record the screen with a supported desktop setup, or import a recording into the editor. [Cursorful features](https://cursorful.com/features) | Upload an approved screen recording and ask for a cut, close-up, caption change or revision. [Valmera upload and edit](https://valmera.io/docs/getting-started) |
| One correction after review | Cursorful documents a local MCP connection for editing the open project, including timeline changes and undo. [Cursorful agent features](https://cursorful.com/features#agents) | Revise the draft in Studio or over hosted MCP, then export the reviewed version. [Valmera recorded revision](https://valmera.io/examples/rough-cut-revision) |
| A marketing MP4 | Cursorful lists local MP4 rendering and commercial use under Pro. Its features page describes the free plan as non-commercial. [Cursorful export terms](https://cursorful.com/features#export) | Confirm eligible recording or editing access before running the job. The public sample MP4 and action record are available to play first. [Valmera access](https://valmera.io/subscribe) |

Valmera publishes this guide. Cursorful capabilities above come from its documentation. The recorded files in the next section are Valmera examples, with no measured Cursorful output.

## Test whether the click actually happened

Give both workflows the same target, stop point and delivery brief. For a recorder you operate, perform these steps yourself. For an agent-operated capture, send the steps as the brief.

> Start at https://valmera.io/docs/getting-started. Show the heading. Open Captions from the Docs sidebar, then hold on the new heading long enough to read it. Keep the cursor in view and deliver a landscape MP4. Before export, show the action and its result in the preview. If the result disappears too soon, keep the opening and extend only the hold on the destination.

A cursor highlight shows where to look. The result proves the action reached the right page. In this [recorded 13-second attempt](https://valmera.io/examples/website-capture/first-attempt.mp4), the cursor moves but the Getting Started guide is still visible after the attempted click. In the [successful 10.83-second recording](https://valmera.io/examples/website-capture/public-docs-walkthrough.mp4), the event log reports the click at 3.57 seconds and the Captions heading is visible at 4.00 seconds. Inspect both recordings with the [matching event record](https://valmera.io/examples/website-capture/evidence.json).

| Handoff item | Check before accepting the file |
| --- | --- |
| Capture | Find the cursor, the intended control and the resulting heading in order. If the video shows a link with no click event, check the destination frame anyway. |
| Revision | Confirm the requested hold changed. Reopen the earlier export and compare the opening and click timing. Save the two versions under separate names. |
| Final MP4 | Play the file on the device and at the size your customer will see it. Confirm that the sidebar and destination text are readable. Keep the source and edit notes if someone will redo the walkthrough after a release. |
