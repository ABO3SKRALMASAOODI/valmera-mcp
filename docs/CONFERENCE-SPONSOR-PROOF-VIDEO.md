# Edit a sponsor proof video from recorded conference footage

A sponsor proof cut gives a partner a short video showing what happened at the conference. Build it around the agreed placement, demonstration or on-stage acknowledgement. Start with the recorded evidence, confirm the audience and rights, then add the approved sponsor artwork. A logo can identify the sponsor, but the footage is what shows the activation happened.

If you are also commissioning an event recap and three vertical social clips, write the sponsor proof as its own deliverable. That makes five videos before separate caption files or alternate crops. The recap provides a story for anyone who missed the event. The speaker clips each need a complete thought and enough context to stand alone. A proof cut needs an approval from the person responsible for the sponsor commitment.

## Match the promise to the recording

| Agreed placement or activity | Evidence to find in footage | Edit check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-stage acknowledgement | The full spoken acknowledgement and the slide or signage visible at that moment | Listen through the name, keep the context, and check the approved spelling in captions. |
| Branded session | A wide shot that shows the room or stage, then a readable shot of the agreed placement | Confirm the sign or slide belongs to that session. Do not describe a future placement as delivered. |
| Product demonstration | A shot of the actual activity plus enough context to establish where and when it happened | Check claim wording and participant clearances. Omit audience or customer close-ups that are not cleared for the intended use. |
