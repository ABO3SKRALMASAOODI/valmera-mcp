Replace bracketed fields. These are instructions to adapt, not recorded results.

FIRST EDIT
Use [recording name or project]. Make a [format and approximate length] video
for [audience] that helps them [understand or do one thing]. Build the story
around [specific moments or quoted lines]. Keep [required explanation,
qualification, demonstration or ending]. Use [caption and visual treatment];
keep [audio preference]. Inspect the recording, make the edit and show me a
preview. Explain any requirement you could not fulfill and identify the
version I am reviewing.

FOCUSED REVISION — USE AFTER REVIEWING THE FIRST PREVIEW
In [preview version], at [time on that preview], change [one thing] to
[desired result]. The moment is [exact phrase or visible action]. Preserve
[approved cuts, other captions, framing and sound]. Render the revised version
and identify any additional change. If this restores or removes footage,
move later timed elements with their intended source moments.

DELIVERY — USE AFTER APPROVING A VERSION
Export the version I approved: [project and preview/version]. Use [required
dimensions, format and delivery conditions]. Give me the completed download,
identify the exported version and report its total duration and any branding.
Do not substitute a different draft.
