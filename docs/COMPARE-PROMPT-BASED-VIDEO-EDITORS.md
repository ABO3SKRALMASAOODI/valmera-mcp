# Compare AI video editors that take text prompts

Start with the footage and the change you need. To shorten an interview, test how the editor selects and arranges your recorded speech. To replace the background behind the speaker, test how well it preserves the person, lighting and motion. Ask for a second change and check the downloaded result before buying.

Valmera publishes this guide and makes one of the editors below. The comparison uses provider documentation checked November 23, 2026. The Valmera example includes an original recording and an exported revision you can inspect.

## Run the same two requests before you subscribe

Use the [25-second source recording](https://valmera.io/examples/trim-and-reframe/original-source.mp4) and pick an editor that can assemble and revise it. This fixture is silent so you can isolate the cuts and framing. For interview work, repeat the test on a short sample with a spoken qualification and review the captions and audio separately.

1. **First cut.** Keep source seconds 3–7, 12–16 and 19–23 in that order. Frame the three shots as a square. Send a preview with the resulting duration. Expect 12 seconds of edited footage.
2. **One revision.** In that version, make the middle shot end at source second 14. Keep the exact first and last source ranges and the approved square framing. Send a new preview and export that version. Expect 10 seconds of edited footage. The last shot now starts at the six-second mark instead of eight.
3. **Check delivery.** In the downloadable video, play the join into the last shot. Verify that the last shot begins at source second 19, both outside shots retain their four seconds, and the crop still follows the approved framing. Note what the editor adds after the program. Save the approved project or preview version alongside the export.
